# Scenario Practice

1. A teammate reports a problem related to helm & release management. What do you inspect first, and how do you verify the cause?

3. You must automate a repetitive helm & release management task safely. How would you handle errors, least privilege, logging, and validation?

For every answer, cover diagnosis, safe remediation, verification, and prevention.
